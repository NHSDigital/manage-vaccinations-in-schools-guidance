A ‘movers out’ notice tells you that a child who was in your cohort has moved out of your area into another SAIS team’s area.

## Reviewing movers out

To see if any children have moved out of your area:

1. Go to **Review** in the main navigation.
2. Select **Movers out**.

![Screenshot of page showing children who have moved school out of SAIS team area.](/assets/images/review-movers-out.png 'Mavis shows a list of all the children who have moved out of SAIS team area.')

## Dismissing movers out

You can dismiss a movers out notice once you have seen and, if necessary, acted on it.

Dismissing the notice removes it from the movers out list for your team. It will still be visible on the child record.
